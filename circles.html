Don't search alone. Search with people near you.

A peer circle is a small group of 4–8 senior people between roles who meet every week to share leads, practise interviews and keep each other going. Matching works by place first, so your circle knows your local job market, your employment rules and your timezone, instead of defaulting to US advice.

Inspired by the Job Search Councils of Never Search Alone.

How circles work

Small

4–8 people, so everyone gets airtime every week.

Local first

Same city where possible: shared recruiters, salary bands, employment law and a table you can actually sit around.

Regular

Weekly or fortnightly, often for about 8 weeks. Each meeting covers wins, blockers and one specific ask from each person.

Private by design

Only organizers are listed. Members aren't named anywhere, and you ask to join privately by email or form.
